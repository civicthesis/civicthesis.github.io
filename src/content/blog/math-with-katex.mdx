Mathematics doesn't have to be cold and rigid. By combining **KaTeX** with our "Active Whiteboard" design system, we can make equations feel like they were just scribbled on a napkin during a eureka moment.

## 1. The "Hand-Drawn" Look

All equations on this site automatically use the **Kalam** font and have a slight organic feel.

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$

## 2. Colorful Equations

We can style specific parts of the equation using CSS classes. Here, variables are red, operators are yellow, and relations are blue!

<div class="math-colorful">
$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2}
$$
</div>


## 3. Animated Math

Why stop at static text? Let's make the math float!

<div class="math-animate-float">
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^2} = \frac{\pi^2}{6}
$$
</div>

Or give it a heartbeat:

<div class="math-animate-pulse">
$$
\heartsuit = \lim_{x \to \infty} \frac{Love}{Time}
$$
</div>

## 4. Inline Math

It works perfectly for inline equations too, like $E = mc^2$ or $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$, flowing right along with your text.

## How it Works

We use `rehype-katex` to render the LaTeX, and then apply a custom CSS font stack:

```css
.katex {
  font-family: 'Kalam', cursive;
}
```

This simple change completely transforms the reading experience, making complex topics feel more approachable and friendly.
