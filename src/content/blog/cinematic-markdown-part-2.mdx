In Part 1, we explored the basics of cinematic text. Now, let's push the boundaries with advanced animations and styles.

## 1. The Glitch Effect

Perfect for cyberpunk or sci-fi themes. This effect mimics a digital signal distortion.

<CinematicText 
  text="SYSTEM FAILURE" 
  font="mono" 
  animation="glitch" 
  color="#ef4444"
  className="text-4xl font-bold text-center my-12"
/>

## 2. 3D Shadows

Give your text weight and presence.

<CinematicText 
  text="HEAVY IMPACT" 
  font="bold-sans" 
  shadow="3d" 
  animation="scale-up"
  className="text-5xl text-center my-12 text-white"
/>

## 3. Elegant Reveals

For a more sophisticated look, use the "tracking-in" animation which expands the letter spacing as the text fades in.

<CinematicText 
  text="Elegance is the only beauty that never fades." 
  font="cinematic" 
  animation="tracking-in" 
  shadow="soft"
  className="text-2xl text-center my-12 italic"
/>

## 4. Kinetic Energy

Sometimes you need to shake things up.

<CinematicText 
  text="WARNING: UNSTABLE" 
  font="mono" 
  animation="shake" 
  color="#eab308"
  className="text-3xl font-bold text-center my-12"
/>

## Combining Effects

You can mix and match fonts, animations, and shadows to create unique styles.

<CinematicCard theme="neon" title="NEO-TOKYO">
  <CinematicText 
    text="WAKE UP" 
    font="bold-sans" 
    animation="glitch"

  />
  <p>The city never sleeps. Neither do the data streams.</p>

## Conclusion

With these new tools, your Markdown content can be as dynamic as a motion picture. Experiment with different combinations to find your unique voice.
