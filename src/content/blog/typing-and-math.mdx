Static content is boring. Let's add some motion to our words and equations!

In this post, we'll explore two new components: `<TypingText />` and `<StyledMath />`.

## 1. Typing Animations

Want to simulate a typewriter effect? Or maybe a terminal command being typed out?

### Basic Usage

<TypingText text="Hello, world! I am being typed out." />

### Custom Speed & Delay

You can control the speed and add a start delay.

<TypingText text="I am typing... very... slowly..." speed={150} />

### Scroll Trigger

By default, the animation starts when the element scrolls into view.

<div style="height: 200px; background: var(--color-code-bg); display: flex; align-items: center; justify-content: center; margin: 2rem 0;">
  <TypingText text="You scrolled down to find me!" trigger="visible" />
</div>

## 2. Styled Math Equations

We already love KaTeX for rendering math, but let's make it fit our design system better.

### Hand-Drawn Style

Perfect for that "back of the napkin" look.

<StyledMath style="hand-drawn">
$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2} dx = \sqrt{\pi}
$$
</StyledMath>

### Colorful

Highlight the beauty of math with colors.

<StyledMath style="colorful">
$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$
</StyledMath>

### Neon / Cyberpunk

For a modern, techy feel.

<StyledMath style="neon">
$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) = \hat H \Psi(\mathbf{r},t)
$$
</StyledMath>

## 3. Animated Equations

Let's add some movement to the equations themselves.

### Float

<StyledMath animation="float" style="hand-drawn">
$$
E = mc^2
$$
</StyledMath>

### Pulse

<StyledMath animation="pulse" style="colorful">
$$
\heartsuit = \sin(x)^2 + \cos(x)^2
$$
</StyledMath>

### Slide Up (On Scroll)

This equation will slide up when you scroll to it.

<StyledMath animation="slide-up" trigger="visible">
$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s} = \prod_{p} \frac{1}{1-p^{-s}}
$$
</StyledMath>

### Reveal

A smooth reveal effect.

<StyledMath animation="reveal" trigger="visible" style="hand-drawn">
$$
\zeta(s) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{1}{n^s}
$$
</StyledMath>

## Usage

Just like our images, these components are auto-imported!

```astro
<TypingText text="Type me!" />

<StyledMath style="neon" animation="pulse">
  $$ E = mc^2 $$
</StyledMath>
```

Go ahead and make your content dynamic!
