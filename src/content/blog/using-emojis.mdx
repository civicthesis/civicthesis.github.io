Emojis are a great way to add personality to your writing. In standard Markdown, you can just paste them in:

👋 Hello world! 🚀 Blast off!

But what if you want them bigger? Or bouncing? Or spinning?

We've added a custom `<Emoji>` component to give you superpowers.

## 1. Sizing

Make a statement with giant emojis.

<ScribbleBox>
  <div style="display: flex; gap: 1rem; align-items: center; justify-content: center;">
    <Emoji size="sm">🐜</Emoji>
    <Emoji size="md">🐈</Emoji>
    <Emoji size="lg">🐕</Emoji>
    <Emoji size="xl">🐘</Emoji>
    <Emoji size="2xl">🦕</Emoji>
  </div>
</ScribbleBox>

```astro
<Emoji size="sm">🐜</Emoji>
<Emoji size="md">🐈</Emoji>
<Emoji size="lg">🐕</Emoji>
<Emoji size="xl">🐘</Emoji>
<Emoji size="2xl">🦕</Emoji>
```

## 2. Animations

Bring your content to life with built-in animations.

### Bounce
Great for excitement!
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="bounce">🏀</Emoji>

```astro
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="bounce">🏀</Emoji>
```

### Spin
Perfect for loading states or just for fun.
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="spin">🌍</Emoji>

```astro
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="spin">🌍</Emoji>
```

### Shake
"No way!" or "Alert!"
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="shake">🔔</Emoji>

```astro
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="shake">🔔</Emoji>
```

### Pulse
A subtle heartbeat effect.
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="pulse">❤️</Emoji>

```astro
<Emoji size="3xl" animation="pulse">❤️</Emoji>
```

## 3. Combining with Diagrams

You can even use emojis inside your Mermaid diagrams!
