Tables are essential for data, but they don't have to be boring. We've created a `<CustomTable />` component that brings life to your grids.

## 1. Hand-Drawn Style

Perfect for that "notebook" feel. It uses the Kalam font and sketchy borders.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Task', 'Status', 'Priority']}
  rows={[
    ['Design Mockups', 'Done', 'High'],
    ['Implement API', 'In Progress', 'High'],
    ['Write Tests', 'Pending', 'Medium']
  ]}
  style="hand-drawn"
/>

## 2. Bold Style

For when you need to make a statement. High contrast, thick borders.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Feature', 'Plan A', 'Plan B']}
  rows={[
    ['Storage', '10GB', '100GB'],
    ['Users', '5', 'Unlimited'],
    ['Support', 'Email', '24/7 Priority']
  ]}
  style="bold"
/>

## 3. Colorful Style

Add a splash of color to differentiate your data.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Metric', 'Q1', 'Q2', 'Q3']}
  rows={[
    ['Revenue', '$10k', '$15k', '$22k'],
    ['Growth', '5%', '12%', '18%'],
    ['Churn', '2%', '1.5%', '1%']
  ]}
  style="colorful"
/>

## 4. Animations

You can animate the table entry with `fade` or `slide-up`.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Step', 'Action']}
  rows={[
    ['1', 'Initialize'],
    ['2', 'Load Data'],
    ['3', 'Render']
  ]}
  style="normal"
  animation="slide-up"
/>

## 5. Mix & Match

Combine styles and animations for a unique look. Here's a **Hand-Drawn** table with **Slide-Up** animation.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Idea', 'Feasibility', 'Fun Factor']}
  rows={[
    ['Moon Base', 'Low', 'High'],
    ['Mars Colony', 'Medium', 'High'],
    ['Backyard Tent', 'High', 'Medium']
  ]}
  style="hand-drawn"
  animation="slide-up"
/>

## Usage

Pass `headers` (array of strings) and `rows` (array of arrays of strings).

```astro
<CustomTable 
  headers={['A', 'B']}
  rows={[
    ['1', '2'],
    ['3', '4']
  ]}
  style="hand-drawn"
/>
```
