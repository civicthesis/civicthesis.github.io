Welcome back! In [Part 1](/blog/image-optimizations), we covered the basics of our `OptimizedImage` component. Now, let's dive into the advanced features that will really make your blog stand out.

## 1. Auto-Import Magic

You might have noticed in Part 1 that we didn't need to import the `OptimizedImage` component. 

We've configured our blog layout to automatically provide this component to all MDX files. This keeps your content clean and focused on writing.

## Usage

Just like before, simply use the component directly:

```astro
<OptimizedImage
  src="/path/to/image.jpg"
  alt="Description"
  width={500}
  height={300}
  style="polaroid"
  animation="rotate-in"
  caption="Your caption here"
/>
```
