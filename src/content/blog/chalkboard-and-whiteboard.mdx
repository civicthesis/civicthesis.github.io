Sometimes the best way to explain a concept is to write it on a board. We've added two new board styles to our blog: **Whiteboard** and **Chalkboard**.

## 1. The Whiteboard

The whiteboard style is clean, modern, and uses marker-like colors. It's perfect for diagrams and brainstorming.

<Board type="whiteboard">
  ### Brainstorming Session

  - <ScribbleCheck color="green" /> **Idea 1**: Use AI for good.
  - <ScribbleCheck color="blue" /> **Idea 2**: Make learning fun.
  - <ScribbleCheck color="red" /> **Idea 3**: <ScribbleLine type="strike" color="red">Take over the world.</ScribbleLine> (Maybe later)

  We can highlight important text with a <ScribbleHighlight color="yellow">yellow marker</ScribbleHighlight> or circle key concepts like <ScribbleCircle color="blue">Innovation</ScribbleCircle>.
</Board>

## 2. The Chalkboard

The chalkboard style brings a classic, academic feel. It uses a dark textured background and chalk-like fonts.

<Board type="chalkboard">
  ### Physics 101

  Today we are learning about **Gravity**.

  <StyledMath style="hand-drawn" animation="typewriter" trigger="visible">
  $$
  F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}
  $$
  </StyledMath>

  Note that <ScribbleCircle color="white">G</ScribbleCircle> is the gravitational constant.

  <DiagramArrow direction="down" color="white" /> **Gravity pulls things down!**
</Board>

## 4. Math on the Board

Combining our `StyledMath` component with the `Board` component creates a truly immersive experience.

<Board type="chalkboard">
  Let's solve for $x$:

  <StyledMath style="hand-drawn">
  $$
  x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
  $$
  </StyledMath>

  <ScribbleBox color="yellow">
    Don't forget the quadratic formula!
  </ScribbleBox>
</Board>

## Usage

Wrap your content in a `<Board>` component and use the annotation components freely.

```astro
<Board type="chalkboard">
  # Hello Class!
  <ScribbleLine color="white">Welcome</ScribbleLine> to the lesson.
</Board>
```

Class dismissed!
