We've covered styles and colors. Now, let's get into the details: **Outlines**, **Fonts**, and **Scroll Animations**.

## 1. Sketchy Outlines

The `sketchy-outline` style gives you that hand-drawn border without the messiness of the full sketchy style. It's clean but organic.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Phase', 'Deliverable']}
  rows={[
    ['Discovery', 'User Research Report'],
    ['Design', 'Figma Prototypes'],
    ['Dev', 'Production Code']
  ]}
  style="sketchy-outline"
  font="hand-drawn"
/>

## 2. Sketchy Colorful Outline

Combine the organic border with your theme colors using `sketchy-colorful-outline`.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Metric', 'Value', 'Trend']}
  rows={[
    ['Users', '1,200', '+15%'],
    ['Sessions', '3,400', '+8%'],
    ['Bounce', '45%', '-2%']
  ]}
  style="sketchy-colorful-outline"
  color="green"
  font="sans"
/>

## 3. Bold & Colorful

For when you need high contrast and impact. The `bold-colorful` style combines thick borders, uppercase headers, and vibrant theme colors.

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Rank', 'Team', 'Points']}
  rows={[
    ['1', 'Red Dragons', '98'],
    ['2', 'Blue Knights', '95'],
    ['3', 'Green Giants', '88']
  ]}
  style="bold-colorful"
  color="red"
  font="sans"
/>

## 4. Font Control

You're not stuck with the hand-drawn font. Use the `font` prop to switch between `hand-drawn`, `sans`, `serif`, and `mono`.

### Sans-Serif (Clean)

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Name', 'Email']}
  rows={[
    ['John Doe', 'john@example.com'],
    ['Jane Smith', 'jane@example.com']
  ]}
  style="minimal"
  font="sans"
  color="blue"
/>

### Monospace (Code)

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Variable', 'Type', 'Default']}
  rows={[
    ['isLoading', 'boolean', 'false'],
    ['data', 'object', 'null'],
    ['error', 'string', 'undefined']
  ]}
  style="bold"
  font="mono"
  color="purple"
/>

## 5. Scroll-Triggered Animations

By default, animations play on load. Set `trigger="visible"` to play them when the table scrolls into view.

Scroll down to see these animate in!

### Slide Up on Scroll

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Event', 'Date']}
  rows={[
    ['Launch', 'Jan 1st'],
    ['Update', 'Feb 15th']
  ]}
  style="sketchy-colorful-outline"
  color="orange"
  animation="slide-up"
  trigger="visible"
/>

<div style="height: 20vh;"></div>

### Zoom In on Scroll

<CustomTable 
  headers={['Feature', 'Status']}
  rows={[
    ['Dark Mode', 'Done'],
    ['API', 'Pending']
  ]}
  style="sketchy-outline"
  animation="zoom-in"
  trigger="visible"
/>
