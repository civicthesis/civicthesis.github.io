### Common Laws
1.  **Sum Law:** The limit of a sum is the sum of the limits.
2.  **Product Law:** The limit of a product is the product of the limits.
3.  **Quotient Law:** The limit of a quotient is the quotient of the limits (if denominator is not 0).
