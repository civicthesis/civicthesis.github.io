A proposition is the basic building block of logic. It is a declarative statement that is either true or false.

## Examples

1.  **True Proposition:** The Earth orbits the Sun.
2.  **False Proposition:** 1 + 1 = 3.
3.  **Not a Proposition:** x > 5 (depends on x).
