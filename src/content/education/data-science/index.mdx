# Welcome to the Data Science Program

This program is designed to create a strong foundation in Data Science and Applications.

## Curriculum Structure

The curriculum is divided into three levels:
1.  **Foundation Level** (8 courses)
2.  **Diploma Level** (6 courses + 2 projects)
3.  **Degree Level** (Advanced electives)

## Subjects in Foundation Level

*   Mathematics 1 & 2
*   Statistics 1 & 2
*   Computational Thinking
*   Introduction to Python
*   English 1 & 2
