In the **OG Crew**, Alan Fang is the resident "Anti-Hero" and professional punching bag. If the crew were a circus, Alan would be the clown who is secretly plotting to replace the lion tamer’s whip with a wet noodle just to see what happens.

<AnimatedCircleImage src="/images/alan-fang.png" alt="Alan Fang" size="200px" />

### 1. The "Unhinged" Intellectual

Alan is a filmmaker and editor by trade, which means he spends too much time looking at frames and not enough time looking at the social consequences of his words.

*   **The Vibe:** He has the energy of a man who has just drunk his fifth cup of coffee and realized that nothing in the universe actually matters, so he might as well say the most <ScribbleHighlight color="yellow">"out of pocket"</ScribbleHighlight> thing possible.
*   **The Dark Twist:** His humor is often described as "unhinged." He specializes in the kind of jokes that make the rest of the crew stop, look at the camera, and pray that the YouTube censors are on lunch break.

### 2. The Professional Punching Bag

In the group dynamic, Alan is frequently the target of the roast. However, he leans into it with a level of meta-awareness.

*   **The Logic:** He acts out a "role" where he knows that *we* know that *he* knows he’s being annoying. It’s a multi-layered irony that usually ends with him making a joke so crude it resets the group's moral compass to zero.

### 3. The "Anti-Fame" Persona

His social media handle—**@ihatealanfang**—tells you everything you need to know about his brand.

<Quote variant="minimal" author="@ihatealanfang" role="Instagram">
While most influencers are desperate for you to like them, Alan’s character operates on the premise that he barely likes himself, so why should you?
</Quote>

### Why he is essential

Every dark humor group needs a <ScribbleHighlight color="pink">"wild card"</ScribbleHighlight>—the person who isn't afraid to be the villain of the episode. Alan doesn't just cross the line; he asks the line if it wants a drink and then roasts its outfit.

<Quote variant="centered" author="The Audience" role="Comment Section">
Alan doesn't tell jokes to make you laugh; he tells jokes to see if he can make the air in the room get 5 degrees colder.
