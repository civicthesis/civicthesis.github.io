Every winter, a thick blanket of grey smog descends upon New Delhi, turning the breath of life into a health hazard.

## Types of Pollution

It's a toxic cocktail:
1.  **Vehicular Emissions**: Millions of cars idling in traffic.
2.  **Stubble Burning**: Smoke drifting from farms in neighboring states.
3.  **Construction Dust**: The endless urge to build upwards.

## Living in the Gas Chamber

For residents, air purifiers have become as essential as water filters. Schools close, construction halts, but the air remains stubborn.

> "We are debating the color of the smoke while the house burns."

Is there a sustainable solution, or is Delhi destined to be the capital of coughs?
