The Aravali range is not just a geological feature; it's the green lung of the National Capital Region. However, it faces an existential threat.

## The Threat of Mining

Despite Supreme Court bans, illegal mining continues to tear into the hills, destroying biodiversity and disrupting the water table.

*   **Loss of Habitat**: Leopards and hyenas are venturing into cities as their forest shrinks.
*   **Dust Storms**: Without the natural barrier of the hills, dust storms from Rajasthan are becoming more frequent in Delhi.

## The Resistance

Citizen groups and environmental activists are fighting back, documenting violations and petitioning the courts. It's a race against time to save what remains of this ancient barrier.
