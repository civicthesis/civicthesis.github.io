Prashant Kishor (PK) argues that the money to fix Bihar’s flood problem already exists within the system, but it is currently being <ScribbleHighlight color="pink">"drained away"</ScribbleHighlight> by corruption and poor policy. His financial blueprint for the **River Grid System** rests on four main pillars.

### 1. Ending the "Relief-Contractor" Loophole

PK’s most scathing financial critique is of the **annual flood relief budget**. Every year, Bihar spends thousands of crores on patching up embankments that inevitably break.

<Quote author="Prashant Kishor" role="Jan Suraaj">
This is a 'leaking bucket' economy. We are pouring money into temporary fixes instead of building a permanent asset.
</Quote>

He argues that by shifting from **Disaster Relief** to **Disaster Prevention**, the state can redirect the recurring annual expenditure of <ScribbleHighlight color="yellow">₹10,000–₹15,000 crore</ScribbleHighlight> into a one-time capital investment for the River Grid.

### 2. The "Liquor Ban" Revenue

A central part of his financial plan is lifting the liquor ban in Bihar. The math is simple but controversial:

*   **The Loss:** Government loses roughly **₹15,000 crore** in annual tax revenue.
*   **The Plan:** This revenue won't go to the general treasury. It will be legally earmarked for **infrastructure and education**.

<Aside type="tip" title="The Seed Fund">
This predictable annual flow of ₹15,000 crore would provide the "seed money" to service massive long-term loans required for the river grid.
</Aside>

### 3. Monetizing Water as an "Economic Asset"

The River Grid isn't just a drainage system; PK views it as a profit-making enterprise.

*   **Hydropower:** Generating electricity through small-scale dams.
*   **Inland Fisheries:** Creating massive reservoirs for commercial fish farming.
*   **Irrigation Tax Value:** Converting drought-prone South Bihar into a multi-crop fertile zone to expand the state's GDP.

### 4. Transparent Global Financing

PK has accused the current administration of "mismanaging" global loans. His proposal involves:

1.  **Long-term Sovereign Bonds:** Issuing "Bihar Development Bonds" specifically for the River Grid.
2.  **Direct Monitoring:** Creating a transparent, third-party monitored fund to prevent leakage to the "contractor nexus".
