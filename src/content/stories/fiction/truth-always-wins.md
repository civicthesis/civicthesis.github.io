Hans and Franz were simple boys. They liked schnitzel, they liked efficiency, and for reasons that baffled their social workers, they really, *really* liked the aesthetic of the 1930s.

"It was about the *branding*, Franz!" Hans argued, adjusting the aperture on his second-hand Leica. "The uniforms! The symmetry! Ignoring the... *everything else*... you have to admit, the visual identity was strong."

"Ja, ja," Franz grunted, sweeping dust off the floor of their new venture: *The Eagle’s Eye Studio*. It was a basement in Berlin that smelled faintly of cabbage and regret. "But we have zero customers, Hans. We cannot eat branding."

Their first customer arrived ten minutes later. She wore a trench coat, smoked a cigarette despite the 'No Smoking' sign, and introduced herself as Greta from *The Daily Truth*.

"I need photos," she spat, tossing a crumpled map on the desk. "The new Trump Bridge. It collapsed yesterday. The contractor used papier-mâché instead of concrete. I need proof."

"Trump... bridge?" Hans frowned. "The one built by that loud American tycoon?"

"The very same. He promised it would be 'Huge'. It was huge, until gravity got involved. Go. Now."

The boys grabbed their gear. A job was a job.

***

 The bridge was a mess. A twisted spine of orange steel lying in the river. Hans was snapping shots of a suspiciously cardboard-looking support beam when Franz screamed.

"A body! Hans, a body!"

Lying amidst the rubble was a man in a grey suit. Hans zoomed in. "Is he...?"

The 'body' suddenly sat up, checked its watch, and sprinted into a storm drain.

"He is late!" Franz gasped. "Follow him! A resurrection is great for the portfolio!"

They chased the man deep into the sewers. Down, down, down, until the smell of sewage was replaced by the smell of... sulfur? And expensive cologne?

They emerged onto a balcony overlooking a cavernous hall. It looked suspiciously like a movie set. Gold statues, red banners, and in the center, a large pit of bubbling, boiling lava.

"Where are we?" Franz whispered.

"Welcome to New Germania," a voice boomed.

Standing on a podium, stroking a white cat that looked very uncomfortable, was a man with a very familiar mustache.

"No way," Hans breathed. "Is that...?"

"Hitler?" Franz squeaked. "But he died! History books said so!"

"Fake news!" The man shouted, his voice echoing. "I have been here! Waiting! Watching! The German economy? Falling! Religious tensions? Rising! People are angry! And who will they turn to? ME!"

Below, an army of men in grey jumpsuits cheered.

"We love you!" one shouted.

"Prove it!" Hitler pointed to the lava. "Jump!"

"Jawohl!" Without hesitation, ten men leaped into the magma with a cheerful "We will be successful!" *Sizzle.*

Franz turned pale. "Hans... I think our admiration for the 'aesthetic' was a mistake."

"You think?" Hans was frantically clicking his shutter. "This is bad. This is very bad."

Suddenly, a side door opened. Two men walked in. One had orange hair and a very long tie. The other was shirtless and riding a bear, wearing a policeman’s badge that said 'Sheriff Putin'.

"The bridge is down, Adolf," the orange-haired man said. "Insurance fraud is a go. We make billions. Best collapse ever. Everyone says so."

"Good, Donald," Hitler nodded. "And the crowd control, Officer Vlad?"

"Under control," the shirtless policeman grunted. "Anyone who complains gets sent to... Gulag. Or Detroit."

"Perfect!" Hitler threw his hands up. "Soon, we will rule the world! Not with tanks, but with bad infrastructure and confusing tweets!"

"Okay," Hans whispered, "We have the proof. We leave. Now."

They sneaked out, lenses caped, hearts pounding.

***

The Berlin Police Station was quiet. The Sergeant looked at the photos.

Photo 1: Hitler pointing at lava.
Photo 2: Donald holding a bag of money labeled 'Bridge Fund'.
Photo 3: Sheriff Putin arm-wrestling a bear on government property.

"This..." The Sergeant's eyes went wide. "This explains the traffic on the Autobahn."

He grabbed the phone. "Get me the UN. And a priest."

***

The sun was setting over Berlin. Hans and Franz walked down the street. They had been arrested immediately after submitting the photos because, technically, breaking into a secret underground Nazi lava lair was trespassing. They wore black-and-white striped prison uniforms, which, honestly, Hans thought were quite slimming.

"We did it, Hans," Franz said, kicking a pebble. "We saved the world."

"And we got the shot," Hans smiled. "Truth always wins, Franz."

As they walked away, the camera panned out. The background music swelled—a triumphant synth-pop track.

*We will be success one day... because truth always success!*

The frame froze as they jumped in the air, freeze-frame high-five style.

**THE END**
