It was a Tuesday, largely because Tuesday sounds like a good day for a historical announcement. The Prime Minister, **Narendra Modiji**, stood before the nation, his beard flowing with the wisdom of a thousand unscheduled press conferences.

The nation held its breath. Was it Demonetization 2.0? Was oxygen being made mandatory for Aadhar linkage? No. It was bigger.

## The Universal Teleprompter

The problem with Earth was simple: too many unscripted questions (mostly from the wind, birds, and occasional foreign journalists). Space offered the ultimate safe space. A vacuum. No sound, no questions.

Modiji’s team developed the **Quantum Teleprompter (QT-56)**. It didn't just display text; it projected his inner monologue directly into the minds of voters.

When the teleprompter malfunctioned during a rally on Mars (Uttar Pradesh East), Modiji simply paused for 14 minutes. The silence was hailed as "Masterstroke Meditation." News channels debated the profound meaning of his blink rate for three days.

## The Global Hug Tour (Intergalactic Edition)

Having hugged every world leader on Earth (including a wax statue of himself by mistake), Modiji set his sights on aliens.

He traveled to Alpha Centauri on the **Vande Bharat Rocket Express** (flagged off by a green flag that cost more than the engine). Upon meeting the Alien High Commander, Modiji went for the signature bear hug.

The Alien, a gelatinous blob with no shoulders, was confused but touched.
"Did he just check my pocket for black money?" the Alien whispered.
"No," replied his advisor. "He’s just finding the camera angle."

And true enough, a camera drone materialized from a wormhole to capture the perfect candid shot.

## Demonetization of Oxygen

Back on Earth, the economy was booming in powerpoint presentations. To boost it further, the Finance Ministry introduced **O2-GST**.

"Why should breathing be free?" Modiji argued in a 4 AM broadcast. "If you are patriotic, you will hold your breath for the nation."

Opposition leaders tried to protest, but their mics were muted in Parliament, and then physically muted by the lack of oxygen. The GDP contracted, but the **Gross Domestic Positivity** reached an all-time high.

## The Final Election

In 2029, the Election Commission announced that elections would no longer be held via EVMs. Instead, they would be held via **Telepathy**.

"If you think of voting for anyone else," the Home Minister warned, "we will know. And your thoughts are not end-to-end encrypted."

Modiji won pending 110% of the vote. The extra 10% came from the Aliens in Alpha Centauri, who were promised a Double Engine Government (one engine on the rocket, one on the tax bill).

As Modiji looked out from his space station, sipping mushroom tea, he sighed. "Where to next?"
The camera zoomed in. The background music swelled.
"The Sun," he whispered. "It needs a little more... saffron."
