**Berlin, 1930s (but also somehow not)**

Hans and Fritz were two photographers who possessed three things in abundance: virginity, poverty, and a misguided admiration for a screaming man with a toothbrush mustache.

Their studio, "Eagle Eye Snaps," was located in a basement that smelled of developing fluid and cabbage. On the wall hung a poster of Adolf Hitler.

"Look at that jawline, Fritz," Hans sighed, adjusting his suspenders which were holding up trousers three sizes too big. "That is the face of a man who will fix the price of bread."

"He speaks with such passion," Fritz agreed, polishing a lens with his only clean sock. "Surely a man who shouts that loud must be telling the truth."

Their reverie was broken by the doorbell. It was a woman. She was wearing a trench coat and smoking a cigarette like she was angry at it. This was Helga, a reporter for *The Daily Truth*, a newspaper that was currently being used mostly to wrap fish.

"I have a job," Helga said, throwing 50 Marks on the table. The boys looked at the money like it was an alien artifact. "The Trump-Brücke collapsed last night. The builder, Donald Trump, says it was 'Windmills' or 'Fake Gravity.' I know he used sawdust instead of cement. I need proof."

---

### The Bridge

The Trump-Brücke was a disaster. It was a golden bridge that now lay in the river Spree like a wet noodle. Standing on the wreckage was a man with orange skin and a tie so long it trailed in the mud.

"This is a witch hunt!" Donald Trump shouted to a group of construction workers. "I build the best bridges. Huge bridges. Beautiful bridges. The water... the water was too wet. Nobody knew water could be this wet."

Hans and Fritz hid behind a pile of rubble.

"Click it, Hans! Click it!" Fritz whispered.

As Hans adjusted the focus, they stumbled over something soft. A body. A man in a suit, lying face down.

"Oh god, a murder!" Hans squeaked.

They frantically took photos of the corpse, the bridge, and Trump pointing at a seagull and calling it a 'loser.' Suddenly, the corpse sneezed.

"Gesundheit," Fritz said instinctively.

The 'corpse' sat up, rubbing his head. "Did they leave? I was just taking a nap. Being a corrupt building inspector is tiring work."

Before the boys could process this, a black van screeched up. Men in masks grabbed the inspector. Hans and Fritz, caught in the momentum of stupidity, chased after them, jumped into the back of their own sputtering motorcycle sidecar, and followed the van.

---

### The Underground Lair

The chase led them to an abandoned beer hall. The van drove into a secret tunnel. Hans and Fritz followed on foot, tumbling down a garbage chute that slid them straight into a massive underground auditorium.

It was terrifying. It was magnificent. It looked like the set of a high-budget opera directed by a madman. In the center of the hall was a giant pit filled with bubbling, boiling red liquid.

"Is that... tomato soup?" Fritz asked.

"No, idiot. It’s lava," Hans whispered. "In Berlin? That’s impressive engineering."

On a high throne sat *Him*. The man from their poster. Adolf. But he wasn't shouting about fixing roads. He was laughing maniacally. Next to him stood Donald Trump and a shirtless policeman riding a bear—Chief of Police, Vladimir Putin.

"The economy is crashing!" Adolf cackled, rubbing his hands. "The Mark is worthless! The people are hungry! Religious groups are fighting in the streets!"

"Excellent," Putin said, cracking his knuckles. "Chaos is a ladder. And I have greased the rungs."

"And the bridge collapse is great for ratings," Trump added. "We will rebuild it. Make Germany Great Again. But first, we destroy it."

Adolf stood up, addressing a legion of soldiers standing by the lava pit.

"My loyal stormtroopers! To prove your loyalty to the chaos... JUMP!"

Without hesitation, five men yelled, "Heil!" and swan-dived into the lava. They sizzled instantly, but they died smiling.

"See?" Adolf turned to Trump and Putin. "They are angry. They are desperate. I will use their anger. I will burn this country to the ground so they beg me to rule over the ashes! Absolute power requires absolute misery!"

Hans and Fritz stared in horror. The poster on their wall didn't mention the lava. Or the intentional destruction of the economy.

"Hans," Fritz whimpered. "I think... I think we might be the baddies' fans."

"We need proof," Hans said, his hands shaking. "The world must know."

He raised his camera. *Click.* The flash went off. It was as bright as a supernova in the dark hall.

Every head turned.

"Run," Fritz said.

---

### The Escape and The Victory

What followed was a chase sequence involving a unicycle, a tray of bratwursts, and a very confused bear. They narrowly escaped through a ventilation shaft, clutching the camera like it was the Holy Grail.

They ran straight to the Berlin Police Headquarters.

"We have it!" Hans screamed, slamming the photos on the desk. "Proof! Trump built the bridge with sawdust! Putin is protecting him! And Hitler... Hitler wants to destroy us all to become King!"

The Desk Sergeant looked at the photos. He looked at the boys. He looked at the photo of Putin riding a bear in the underground lair.

"This is shocking," the Sergeant said, his face unreadable. "Truly shocking evidence of corruption."

"So you will arrest them?" Fritz asked, hope shining in his eyes.

"Justice," the Sergeant said, stamping a piece of paper, "always prevails."

---

### The Ending

The scene cuts to black.

Music fades in—a somber, orchestral version of *"We Shall Overcome"* ( *Hum Honge Kamyab*).

**EXT. BERLIN STREET - DAY**

Hans and Fritz are walking down the cobblestone street.

They are not wearing medals. They are wearing black-and-white striped prison uniforms. Heavy iron balls are chained to their ankles. They are carrying pickaxes.

Passersby spit on them. A newspaper headline blows past in the wind: **"TWO SPIES ARRESTED FOR SABOTAGING TRUMP-BRÜCKE. HERO LEADER ADOLF PROMISES SAFETY."**

Hans looks at Fritz. They are tired, broken, and going to prison for life.

"We did it, Fritz," Hans says, a delusional smile on his face. "We submitted the proof. The truth is in the file."

"Ja, Hans," Fritz replies, adjusting his heavy cap. "The truth always wins. We are very successful today."

They march into the fog, dragging their chains, humming the tune of victory as the camera pans up to a giant billboard of Hitler, Trump, and Putin shaking hands, watching over the city.

**FADE OUT.**
