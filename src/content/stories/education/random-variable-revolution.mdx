Welcome to the first part of our deep dive into the **IIT Madras BS Degree Foundation Statistics 2** course. 

In Stats 1, we dealt with datasets. We looked at past events, calculated means, and plotted histograms. But in Stats 2, we move from the *past* to the *possible*. We enter the world of **Random Variables (RVs)**.

## What is a Random Variable?

Think of a Random Variable not as a "value," but as a **function**. It maps the outcomes of a random experiment (like tossing a coin or rolling a die) to real numbers.

> [!NOTE]
> A Discrete Random Variable is one that can take only a countable number of values.

### The Probability Mass Function (PMF)

For discrete RVs, we use a PMF to describe the probability of each outcome. The PMF $P(X = x)$ must satisfy two conditions:
1. $P(x) \geq 0$ for all $x$.
2. $\sum P(x) = 1$.

## Why does this matter?

Understanding the distribution is the foundation for everything that follows: Normal distributions, Sampling, and Hypothesis testing. If you can't define your RV, you can't do the math.
