If you check the official notification for Haryana CET Group C, the "Qualifying Marks" seem approachable: **50% for General** and **40% for Reserved** categories.

But here is the hard truth: scoring the qualifying marks makes you *eligible*, not *selected*. The gap between "Passing" and "Getting a Job" is massive.
