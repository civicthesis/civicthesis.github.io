A common question from aspirants is: *"What was the Clerk cut-off for the last 5 years?"*

The answer is complicated because **Haryana CET is new.** It was first held in **2022**, meaning a 5-year history of "CET Cut-offs" simply doesn't exist. Before 2022, HSSC conducted recruitment differently.

## 2. Clerk-Specific Data Points

While official "Clerk-Only" lists are rare, we found specific data points buried in shortlisting PDFs.

*   **Post Code 380 (Clerk):** In the Group 56-57 shortlisting, the unreserved cut-off was observed around <Highlight>65.69 Marks</Highlight>.
*   **Technical Posts:** Group 6 lists show cut-offs ranging from 60–72, placing Clerk firmly in the **Mid-60s to High-70s** competitive band.
