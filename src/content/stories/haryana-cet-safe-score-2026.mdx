In the high-stakes game of Haryana CET Group C, there is a dangerous trap: aiming for the *expected* cut-off.

History shows clearly: **A Safe Score is generally 10–15 marks ABOVE your category’s expected cut-off.** For a popular, non-technical post like **Clerk**, this reality pushes the target significantly higher.
