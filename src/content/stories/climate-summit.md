The urgency is palpable. Delegates from over 100 nations have convened to discuss the pressing issue of climate change.

## Ambitious Goals

The summit has produced a new agreement aiming for net-zero emissions by 2040. This is a decade earlier than previous targets.

1.  **Renewable Energy Transition**: massive investment in solar and wind.
2.  **Reforestation**: committing to planting 1 trillion trees.
3.  **Ocean Protection**: expanding marine protected areas.

While skepticism remains, the unified front presented at this summit offers a glimmer of hope for the planet's future.
