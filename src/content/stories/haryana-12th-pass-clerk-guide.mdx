If you have passed your **12th (10+2)** and are looking for a government job in Haryana, the **HSSC Group C Clerk** exams are your biggest opportunity. The gateway to these jobs is the **Common Eligibility Test (CET)**.

Here is the best part: **No negative marking**. But don't let that fool you—competition is fierce, and the "Haryana GK" section is the game-changer.

---

## 1. Eligibility & The "No Negative Marking" Rule

<Aside type="note" title="Who Can Apply?">
    **Qualification:** 12th Pass (Any stream) from a recognized board.
    **Age Limit:** Generally **18–42 years** (with relaxations for reserved categories).
</Aside>

**The Exam Format:**
*   **Mode:** Offline OMR (Pen & Paper).
*   **Duration:** 1 Hour 45 Minutes.
*   **Questions:** 100 MCQs.
*   **Marking Scheme:** **No negative marking** for wrong answers. However, leaving a question *blank* may attract a small penalty (0.945 marks), so attempt everything!

---

## 2. What Exactly Should You Study?

The exam is split into two distinct zones.

    title="The Golden Ratio: 75:25"

### Zone A: General Subjects (75 Questions)
*   **Reasoning:** Puzzles, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding (High Scoring).
*   **Maths:** Arithmetic focus—Percentages, Profit & Loss, Time & Work.
*   **English & Hindi:** Basic grammar, vocabulary, and error detection.
*   **General Science:** 10th-level Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.
*   **Computer:** MS Office basics are a must.

### Zone B: Haryana GK (25 Questions)
This is where the exam is won or lost. You must master:
*   **History & Culture:** Battles of Panipat, Folk dances, Festivals.
*   **Geography:** Rivers, Canals, Districts.
*   **Polity:** Panchayati Raj, Schemes, CM/Governor lists.
