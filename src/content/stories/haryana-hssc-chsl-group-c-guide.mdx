In Haryana, the "CHSL" (Combined Higher Secondary Level) jobs mainly flow through two pipelines: the **Central SSC CHSL** and the state-level **HSSC Group C Recruitment** (via the Common Eligibility Test or CET).

While the geography differs, the DNA of these exams is strikingly similar: English, Reasoning, Quant, and General Awareness—with one major "Haryana Twist" for the state exam.

---

## 1. The Exam Syllabus (Haryana 10+2)

For the Haryana CET Group C clerk-type prelims, the exam is **100 MCQs** in **105 minutes**. The subjects are fixed with a clear question-wise weightage.

### Overall Exam Pattern

*   **Mode & Type:** Offline OMR-based written exam, bilingual (Hindi & English).
*   **Duration:** 1 hour 45 minutes (105 minutes).
*   **Questions:** 100 MCQs, each of 1 mark.
*   **Marking System:** **No negative marking** for wrong answers.
    *   *Note:* leaving a question blank may attract a penalty of **0.945 marks**. So, attempt everything!
*   **Qualifying Marks:** 
    *   General: **50%**
    *   Reserved Categories: **40%**
